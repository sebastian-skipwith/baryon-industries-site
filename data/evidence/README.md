# Baryon public Earth evidence

Published within https://baryon.industries/resource-intelligence .

`manifest.json` identifies each source snapshot, publication/acquisition dates, original source hashes, per-file SHA-256 checksums and limitations. The browser verifies each lazy-loaded dataset against its manifest checksum. Soil layers retain every normalized record, including missing and censored assays. Core files are split by hole; methods, source units and flags remain separate.

- USGS Data Series 801: https://pubs.usgs.gov/ds/801/ . Source metadata: https://mrdata.usgs.gov/metadata/ds-801.txt . Attribute USGS; see the source use constraints and qualifications.
- USGS Copperwood Western Syncline: https://doi.org/10.5066/P9LIFRF0 . CC0 as marked by the USGS release. Selected known-deposit samples, not a global discovery test. Reported depths are not established as true vertical and no trajectory is supplied.
- The model result is a saved, consumed regional historical soil-copper audit. It does not establish reserves, deep geology, geographic extrapolation, calibrated point uncertainty, or recursive research-policy improvement. Future tuning must use independent evaluation.

UN Comtrade numerical records are excluded from this release. The source's commercial redistribution requirements have not been satisfied or verified for Baryon. The public UI links to its source and publication policy: https://uncomtrade.org/docs/policy-on-use-and-re-dissemination/ . The original local evidence is preserved.

The export reads only the designated geological file store. It never queries the private founder database or loads credentials. Source observations, missing values, original qualifiers, and the completed experiment scores are checked against the local source artifacts before publication. Export date is not measurement date or source publication date.

The research-registry snapshot records its hash-chain head, original historical result timing, frozen-protocol capabilities and distinct research proposals. The initial historical soil entry was imported after completion; this registry did not preregister that old run. New protocols require explicit grouping, source fingerprints, baseline, candidates, metrics, uncertainty procedure, evaluator identity and a one-use audit budget. The registry does not automatically score or promote models. Full new-plan sample memberships stay local; their fingerprint and counts are public. An external published head is needed to detect replacement of an entire local history.
