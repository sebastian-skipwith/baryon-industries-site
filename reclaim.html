Where this goes

The calculator is step one. The engine is the point.

AI appraisal

The full engine reads a raw listing — "old electrical stuff, must haul away" — and produces the material breakdown, weight ranges, value waterfall (resell → parts → scrap), and verdict automatically.

Sourced pricing

Every benchmark carries a source and an as-of date, so you can see how current a price is before you haul.

Provenance first

Theft-prone material is flagged automatically. Clean ownership is a requirement of the system, not an afterthought.
