The problem

Everything civilization builds begins in the ground.

Energy systems, AI infrastructure, housing, transport, defense — all of it starts as rock. But the way we find and produce the minerals underneath has barely changed in decades. Exploration is slow. Drilling is dangerous and labor-constrained. And the data behind billion-dollar resource decisions moves at the speed of paperwork.

First focus

The Autonomous Exploration Cell.

Baryon's wedge is mineral exploration — automating the highest-friction work at the drill site and turning every meter drilled into structured geological intelligence. Physical sample to updated geological model in hours, not months.

Robotics

Autonomous systems for core drilling operations, rod and core handling, and site work — machines built for the hardest environments in the primary economy.

Intelligence

Telemetry, automated core characterization, and geological AI. Every operating hour compounds into a proprietary understanding of the subsurface.

Resources

Over time, Baryon aligns with outcomes — participating in the resources its systems help discover, not just the equipment that finds them.

The horizon

The endgame is an autonomous resource network: machines that explore, produce, process, move, and build — on this planet, and then on others.
