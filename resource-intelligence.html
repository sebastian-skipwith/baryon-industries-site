What it does

Query the world like you'd ask an analyst.

Ask where the world's cobalt comes from, what a disruption at Hormuz touches, or what materials are inside a product on your desk — and get answers grounded in a sourced database, not a language model's guess. Every row carries its source, its confidence, and its as-of date. The model is deliberately honest about what it doesn't know yet.

The world model

Mines, smelters, refineries, ports, storage terminals, and factories from federal and open datasets — USGS, the World Port Index, Global Energy Monitor — refreshed on schedule.

Live logistics

Daily vessel transits through the world's maritime chokepoints and port-level activity derived from satellite AIS data, via IMF PortWatch.

Machine eyes

Sentinel satellite coverage over key assets, supply-chain route graphs from facility to facility, and an autonomous agent that researches and expands the model around the clock.
